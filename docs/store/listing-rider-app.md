### Full description

> iPrime Rider is the work app for delivery riders in Katsina State. Get matched to nearby jobs, navigate to pickup and dropoff, capture proof of delivery, and watch the money land in your wallet on every trip.

**Earn**
- Get paid per trip - fees shown before you accept
- Live earnings dashboard: today, week, month
- Wallet withdrawal to any Nigerian bank account, usually under 5 minutes
- No subscription, no monthly fees

**Smart job matching**
- Jobs you can actually do: matched on your live location, vehicle type, and rating
- Accept or skip - no penalty for skipping a job you can't take
- Round-robin offer queue; cleared if you don't respond in time so the next rider gets a shot

**On-the-job tools**
- Turn-by-turn navigation to pickup, then to dropoff
- One-tap call to the customer (masked number - your real number stays private)
- In-app chat with the customer and the merchant
- Proof of delivery: photo, signature, or PIN - whatever the trip requires
- Wrong-address flag if dispatch sent you to the wrong place

**Safety**
- Trip GPS recorded for 30 days, then deleted (lets us help if anything goes wrong)
- Operational cancellations (vehicle issue, address wrong) carry no penalty
- 24/7 in-app support; emergency contact one tap away during a live trip

**Get verified**
- Riders complete KYC: BVN, driving licence, vehicle papers, insurance, vehicle photos
- Approval typically within 1–2 working days
- Re-upload expiring documents directly in-app - never miss a job because your insurance lapsed last week

**For riders who already know how to ride**
- iPrime is for riders with a licensed vehicle (motorcycle, scooter, or small car)
- 18+ only
- You are an independent contractor, not an iPrime employee - terms in the rider agreement

- 18+ ne kawai
