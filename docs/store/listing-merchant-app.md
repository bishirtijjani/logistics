business on iPrime - accept orders, manage the shop, get paid.

### Full description

> iPrime Merchant is the work app for shop owners and vendors selling on iPrime in Katsina State. Get your orders the moment they land, accept or decline in one tap, manage your catalogue, and watch the money reach your wallet.

**Never miss an order**
- New-order push with inline **Accept** / **Reject** actions - act straight from the notification tray, no need to open the app
- Live order queue with status at a glance: new, preparing, ready for pickup, delivered
- Order detail with items, customer notes, delivery address, and the rider assigned

**Run your shop**
- Manage products, prices, stock, and availability
- Open or close your store for the day in one tap
- Set your store's pickup point so riders find you fast

**Get paid**
- Every delivered order lands in your merchant wallet
- Track available and pending balance, plus full transaction history
- Withdraw to any Nigerian bank account, usually within minutes
- Earnings and sales analytics - daily, weekly, monthly

**More than orders**
- Pay bills and buy airtime/data directly from the app
- In-app support and dispute handling with the iPrime team
- Marketing tools to reach more customers nearby

**Sell on iPrime**
- Merchants complete a short onboarding and KYC review
- Approval typically within 1-2 working days
- Built for real shop owners - upload your products, set your hours, go live

**Stay secure**
- Biometric lock (Face ID / fingerprint) to protect your account and withdrawals
- 18+ only
- Need to leave? Delete your account in-app at any time
