### Full description

> Logistics Platform is the everyday wallet and delivery app for . One app - pay bills, send money, order food, get medicine delivered, restock your kitchen, and have packages run across town.

**Wallet & money**
- Free NUBAN wallet - get a dedicated bank account number for instant funding
- Send money to anyone with an Logistics Platform account, free
- Withdraw to any Nigerian bank account in minutes
- Six-digit transaction PIN; no transaction moves without it
- Saved beneficiaries and one-tap repeat transfers

**Top up everything**
- Airtime: MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile - usually delivered in seconds
- Mobile data: every plan from every network, fastest sellers up top
- Electricity: AEDC, IBEDC, EKEDC and the rest - receive your token instantly
- Cable TV: DStv, GOtv, Startimes - renew without leaving home

**Order from real shops near you**
- Food from local restaurants, ready in 20–40 minutes
- Pharmacy - prescription required for restricted medication, reviewed before dispense
- Grocery - pick substitution preference (do not substitute, merchant's pick, contact me)
- General retail - supermarkets, fashion, electronics, the whole high street

**Send a package across town**
- Same-day courier, motorcycle delivery
- Live rider tracking with map and ETA
- PIN-on-delivery option for high-value items
- Proof of delivery: photo, signature, OTP

**Built for the way Nigeria actually works**
- Pay-as-you-go - no monthly fees
- Available in English and Hausa
- Customer support inside the app, replies within hours
- Works on slow networks; skeleton loaders, not spinners

Logistics Platform is operated under agreements with licensed Nigerian payment partners. Your wallet balance is held in segregated accounts with regulated banks.

**Permissions we ask for, and why**
- Location: to show options near you, and to track your delivery on the map
- Camera + photo library: for KYC, prescription upload, and dispute evidence
- Notifications: order status, OTPs, money received

**Need help?** Tap *Profile → Help & Support* in the app, or email bishirtijjani247@gmail.com.
