# Customer support FAQ - V1

The 20 most likely questions a user (customer or rider) will ask in the
first weeks after launch, with the answer support should give. Use these
as canned replies and the public help-center seed.

Each entry has both an **EN** and a **HA** answer.

---

## Account & sign-up

### 1. "I didn't get the OTP."

- **EN:** OTPs are sent via SMS and usually arrive within 30 seconds. If after 1 minute you don't see it: (a) check your spam/promotional inbox if it was emailed, (b) tap "Resend OTP" - you can resend every 60 seconds, (c) confirm your phone number is correct and isn't on Do Not Disturb mode. If you've tried 3 times in 2 minutes, wait 5 minutes before trying again - we throttle to prevent abuse.
- **HA:** Ana aika OTPs ta SMS kuma yawanci suna isa cikin daƙiƙa 30. Idan bayan minti 1 ba ku gani ba: (a) duba akwatin spam ɗinku idan an aika ta imel, (b) latsa "Sake aika OTP" - kowane daƙiƙa 60 za ku iya sake aikawa, (c) tabbatar lambar wayar ku daidai ce kuma ba a yanayin Kar a Dame ba. Idan kun gwada sau 3 cikin minti 2, ku jira minti 5 kafin sake gwadawa.

### 2. "I can't verify my BVN."

- **EN:** Check that the BVN is exactly 11 digits and matches the BVN on file with your bank - including the name spelling. If it still fails, request a BVN re-issue from your bank; the most common cause is a mismatch between what your bank has and what you typed. We never store the actual BVN - only a one-way hash.
- **HA:** Ku tabbata BVN ɗin ku daidai ne na lambobi 11 kuma ya yi daidai da BVN ɗin da yake a banki - har da rubutun suna. Idan har yanzu ya kasa, ku nemi sake fitar da BVN daga bankin ku.

### 3. "I forgot my password."

- **EN:** On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. We send a reset link valid for 30 minutes. The link can be used only once.
- **HA:** A allon shiga, latsa "An manta da kalmar sirri?" kuma shigar da imel ɗin ku. Muna aika muku da hanyar haɗi ta sake saitawa wadda ke da inganci na minti 30. Ana iya amfani da hanyar haɗi sau ɗaya kawai.

### 4. "I forgot my transaction PIN."

- **EN:** Open the app → Profile → Security → Change PIN. You'll be asked to verify with a one-time code sent to your registered phone before you can set a new PIN. If you've forgotten the PIN AND lost access to your phone number, contact support - recovery requires manual KYC re-verification.
- **HA:** Buɗe app → Profile → Tsaro → Canza PIN. Za a tambaye ku ku tabbatar da lambar lokaci ɗaya da aka aika zuwa wayar ku da aka yi rajistar.

## Wallet & money

### 5. "I funded my wallet from my bank but the balance hasn't updated."

- **EN:** NUBAN deposits usually credit within 30 seconds. If after 30 minutes the balance hasn't moved: (a) confirm you transferred to your dedicated iPrime NUBAN account number, not someone else's, (b) keep the bank receipt - we'll need the reference. We resolve every funding query; if your money is stuck somewhere, we recover it from KatPay (our payment partner) within 1–2 business days.
- **HA:** Cika NUBAN yawanci yana shiga cikin daƙiƙa 30. Idan bayan minti 30 ma'auni bai canza ba: (a) tabbatar kun canja zuwa lambar NUBAN na iPrime ku ta musamman, ba na wani ba, (b) riƙe rasid ɗin banki - za mu buƙaci tushen rajistar. Muna warware kowace tambaya ta cika; idan kuɗin ku yana makale, muna dawowa daga abokin biyar mu cikin kwanaki 1-2 na aiki.

### 6. "How long does a withdrawal take?"

- **EN:** Withdrawals to any Nigerian bank usually arrive within 5 minutes during business hours (8am–6pm WAT, Mon–Sat). Outside those hours or during a bank network outage, it can take up to 2 hours. If a withdrawal is still pending after 6 hours, contact support - we will trace and resolve.
- **HA:** Cire kuɗi zuwa kowane banki na Najeriya yawanci yana isa cikin minti 5 a lokacin aiki (8 na safe – 6 na yamma WAT, Litinin–Asabar).

### 7. "Is there a fee for withdrawing?"

- **EN:** Withdrawal fee is shown on the Withdraw screen before you confirm. We charge a flat fee (currently ₦25) regardless of the amount - this covers the bank-transfer cost on our side. No hidden fees.
- **HA:** Ana nuna kuɗin cirewa a allon Cirewa kafin ku tabbatar. Muna cajin kuɗi madaidaici (a halin yanzu ₦25) ba tare da la'akari da adadin ba.

### 8. "Can I send money to someone who doesn't have iPrime?"

- **EN:** Yes - use Withdraw to any Nigerian bank account. iPrime-to-iPrime transfers are instant and free; bank transfers carry the ₦25 withdrawal fee.
- **HA:** I - yi amfani da Cirewa zuwa kowane asusun banki na Najeriya. Canja iPrime-zuwa-iPrime nan da nan ne kuma kyauta; canja banki yana ɗaukar kuɗin cirewa na ₦25.

## Orders & delivery

### 9. "How long does delivery take?"

- **EN:** Most deliveries inside Katsina arrive in 25–45 minutes from order confirmation. Food prep takes the merchant 10–20 minutes; the rider trip itself is usually under 20. Live ETA on the tracking screen updates as the rider moves.
- **HA:** Yawancin isarwa a cikin Katsina suna isa cikin minti 25–45 daga tabbatar da odar. Shirye-shiryen abinci yana ɗaukar mai shago minti 10–20; tafiyar mai keke kanta yawanci ƙasa da minti 20.

### 10. "I want to cancel my order."

- **EN:** You can cancel from the order tracking screen any time before pickup. If the merchant has already started preparing, a small cancellation fee may apply (shown in the preview before you confirm). After pickup, cancellation isn't possible - you can refuse at the door, in which case you pay the delivery fee but get a full item refund.
- **HA:** Za ku iya soke daga allon bibiya na odar kowane lokaci kafin ɗaukar. Idan mai shago ya riga ya fara shiri, ƙaramin kuɗin soke yana iya amfani (ana nunawa a samfoti kafin ku tabbatar). Bayan ɗauka, soke ba zai yiwu ba.

### 11. "The wrong item was delivered."

- **EN:** Open the order, tap **Report an issue** → **Wrong item**. Attach a photo of what arrived. Our support team reviews disputes within 4 business hours. If your dispute is upheld, the refund hits your iPrime wallet immediately - no waiting on the bank.
- **HA:** Buɗe odar, latsa **Bayar da rahoton matsala** → **Kuskuren abu**. Haɗa hoton abin da ya iso. Ƙungiyar tallafi mu ta duba gardandami cikin awoyi 4 na aiki.

### 12. "I never got my order."

- **EN:** Open the order → Report an issue → Item not received. Include any context (the rider called and never came, the rider claimed delivery but you didn't receive). We pull the rider's GPS log to confirm; if the trip wasn't actually completed at your address, you get a full refund + a goodwill credit.
- **HA:** Buɗe odar → Bayar da rahoton matsala → Ba a karɓi abu ba. Haɗa kowane mahallin (mai keke ya kira ba a zo ba, mai keke ya ce isar amma ba ku karɓa ba).

### 13. "Can I tip my rider?"

- **EN:** Tipping inside the app isn't supported in V1 - riders earn the full disclosed fare for each trip. You're welcome to tip in cash at the door; this stays between you and the rider.
- **HA:** Ba a tallafawa bayar da kyauta a cikin app a V1 ba - masu keke suna samun cikakken kuɗin tafiya da aka bayyana. Kuna iya bayar da kyauta da kuɗi a ƙofa.

## VTU (airtime, data, electricity, cable)

### 14. "I paid for airtime but the credit didn't arrive."

- **EN:** Airtime usually credits in under 10 seconds. If 5 minutes have passed and it's still not on your line: (a) check Recent Transactions for the transaction status - "Failed" means the network rejected it (rare) and we've already refunded you, "Pending" means the network hasn't confirmed back to us yet - wait 15 minutes, (b) if Pending after 15 minutes, contact support with the transaction reference.
- **HA:** Caji yawanci yana shiga ƙasa da daƙiƙa 10. Idan minti 5 ya wuce kuma har yanzu ba a kan layin ku ba: (a) duba Mu'amalla na Baya don matsayin mu'amalla - "Ya gaza" yana nufin cibiyar sadarwa ta ƙi (ba sabani ba) kuma mun riga mun mayar muku, "Yana jiran tabbatarwa" yana nufin cibiyar sadarwa ba ta tabbatar mana ba tukuna - ku jira minti 15.

### 15. "I bought electricity but never got the token."

- **EN:** Tokens are sent both to the in-app result screen AND to the meter automatically when possible. Open the Utility History tab - the token will be there. If the History shows the transaction succeeded but no token, the meter type (prepaid/postpaid) may be set wrong on your account; contact support with the meter number and we'll re-issue.
- **HA:** Ana aika token zuwa allon sakamako a cikin app DA zuwa mita kai tsaye lokacin da yake yiwuwa. Buɗe Tab ɗin Tarihin Kayan Aikin - token zai kasance a wurin.

## Pharmacy & restricted goods

### 16. "Why do I need a prescription for some pharmacy items?"

- **EN:** Nigerian pharmacy regulations require a valid doctor's prescription for certain medications. iPrime enforces this for any product flagged as prescription-only - you upload a photo of the prescription at checkout, and the pharmacist reviews it before dispensing. Submitting a forged prescription is a criminal offence; we report attempts to the relevant authorities.
- **HA:** Dokokin magunguna na Najeriya suna buƙatar takardar likita mai inganci ga wasu magunguna. iPrime yana tilasta wannan ga kowane samfurin da aka yi alama a matsayin takardar likita kawai - kuna ɗora hoton takardar likita lokacin biya, kuma likitan magani yana dubawa kafin bayarwa.

## Riders

### 17. (Rider) "How are jobs assigned?"

- **EN:** Jobs go to the rider whose current GPS is closest to the pickup, weighted by rating, acceptance rate, and recent activity. Each offer is exclusive to one rider for ~20 seconds before passing to the next. Skipping a job doesn't penalize you - but if you skip many in a row, your offer rate drops automatically until you accept again.
- **HA:** Ayyukan na zuwa ga mai keke wanda GPS na yanzu yake mafi kusa da ɗaukar, an auna shi da ƙimar, ƙimar karɓa, da aiki na baya-bayan nan. Kowane tayi yana keɓance ga mai keke ɗaya na kusan daƙiƙa 20 kafin ya wuce ga na gaba.

### 18. (Rider) "When do I get paid?"

- **EN:** Earnings land in your rider wallet the moment a trip is marked **DELIVERED** and the customer's escrow is released. From the wallet, you can withdraw to any Nigerian bank - usually arrives within 5 minutes. No weekly payroll cycle; cash out whenever you want.
- **HA:** Kuɗin shiga na shiga walat ɗin mai keke ku a daƙiƙa da aka yi alama kan tafiya **AN ISA** kuma an saki adadin kuɗin abokin ciniki.

### 19. (Rider) "A customer says they didn't get the order, but I dropped it off."

- **EN:** Open the trip in your history, tap **Dispute** → **Customer claims non-delivery**. Add your evidence (proof-of-delivery photo, GPS log will be pulled automatically). The dispute team weighs the GPS, photo, OTP entry if any, and the customer's history. If your record is consistent, you win and the customer's claim is dismissed.
- **HA:** Buɗe tafiya a tarihin ku, latsa **Gardandami** → **Mai abokin ciniki ya ce ba a isar ba**. Ƙara shaidar ku (hoton shaidar isarwa, za a ja log ɗin GPS kai tsaye).

## Privacy & security

### 20. "How do I delete my account?"

- **EN:** Profile → Account → Delete Account. We require: (a) wallet balance must be zero or positive (withdraw first if needed), (b) no in-flight orders. After confirmation, your account is deactivated immediately and personal data is deleted within 90 days. We are required by Nigerian financial regulations to keep transaction records for 5 years - these are anonymized but not deleted.
- **HA:** Profile → Asusu → Goge Asusu. Muna buƙatar: (a) ma'aunin walat ɗinku ya zama sifili ko tabbatacce, (b) babu odoji da ke gudana. Bayan tabbatarwa, an cire asusun ku nan take kuma an goge bayanan sirri cikin kwanaki 90.
