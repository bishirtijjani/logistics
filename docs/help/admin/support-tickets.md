# Handling support tickets

The **Support** page (*Support Tickets*) is where customer and merchant help
requests come in. This guide covers picking up a ticket and working it to
resolution.

![Support tickets](/help/screenshots/admin/support.png)

---

## Work a ticket

1. On the **Support Tickets** list, click a ticket to open it. Use **Refresh** to
   pull in new ones.
2. The detail view shows the **Customer**, the **Assignment**, and the **Status**.
3. Click **"Assign to me"** so the team knows you're handling it.
4. Reply to the customer in the conversation, and update the **Status** as you go:
   - **Open** → just arrived
   - **In Progress** → you're working it
   - **Pending User** → waiting on the customer to reply
   - **Resolved** / **Closed** → done

> 💡 Always **assign yourself** before replying so two admins don't work the same
> ticket. Set it to **Pending User** when the ball is in the customer's court so
> it doesn't look unattended.

---

## Tips

- Keep replies clear and friendly — the customer sees them.
- Use an **internal note** (where available) for context you don't want the
  customer to see.
- If a ticket is really about an order dispute or a refund, handle the money side
  under [Disputes](/help/admin/resolving-disputes) or
  [Finances](/help/admin/finance) and link it in your reply.
