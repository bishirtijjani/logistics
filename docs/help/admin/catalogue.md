# Categories, VTU networks & plans

These three screens control what merchants can sell under and what bills
customers can pay - the platform's catalogue and pricing.

---

## Merchant categories

The **Categories** page (*Merchant Categories*) is the master list of categories a
merchant can belong to - Food, Groceries, Pharmacy, Fashion, and so on.

![Merchant categories](/help/screenshots/admin/categories.png)

Create or edit a category with a **name**, **description**, **icon** (pick from
the icon list), and colour. Categories also carry an *attribute schema* that
tells the merchant product form which options (size, colour, pack units) to show.

> ⚠️ Changing or removing a category affects every merchant in it and how their
> products are shown to customers. Edit with care.

---

## VTU networks

The **VTU Networks** page maps the airtime/data/cable/electricity providers to the
IDs our VTU partner uses. You manage these by type so you can update a provider ID
without a code change.

![VTU networks](/help/screenshots/admin/vtu-networks.png)

---

## VTU plans & pricing

The **VTU Plans** page (*VTU Pricing*) is where you manage the actual sellable
items - data bundles, cable packages, and the cost/selling price for each.

![VTU pricing](/help/screenshots/admin/vtu-plans.png)

Use the tabs to switch between **Airtime, Data, Cable, and Electricity**, and add
or edit a plan's name, validity, and price.

> 💡 Selling price vs cost price sets your margin on each bundle. Keep these in
> sync with your VTU provider's current rates so you never sell at a loss.
