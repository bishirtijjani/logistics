# Finances, transactions & withdrawals

Money on the platform is managed across three places: **Transactions** (the
ledger), **Financial** (operations and reconciliation), and the **Withdrawals**
tab on the Merchants page (payouts).

---

## Transactions

The **Transactions** page is the complete record of every money movement.

![Transactions](/help/screenshots/admin/transactions.png)

Filter by **type** - *Payment*, *Refund*, *Delivery Earning*, *Commission*,
*Withdrawal* - and by **status** - *Pending*, *Completed*, *Failed*.

> 💡 Chasing a customer's "my money didn't arrive" report? Filter to their
> transaction type and status here to see exactly what happened.

---

## Financial operations

The **Financial** page (*Financial Operations*) is for platform-level money
management - reconciliation, escrow, and the system wallets that hold funds in
transit between customers, merchants, and riders.

> ⚠️ Actions here affect platform-wide balances. Make sure you understand a
> figure before acting on it, and use the **Transactions** ledger to back up any
> investigation.

---

## Approving withdrawals

Merchant payout requests are approved on the **Merchants → Withdrawals** tab - see
[Approving & managing merchants](/help/admin/managing-merchants#approve-merchant-withdrawals).
You can approve them one at a time or in bulk; rejecting one returns the funds to
the merchant's wallet.

---

## Failed transactions

The **Dashboard** highlights recent **Failed Transactions**. Open one from there
or from the Transactions page to investigate - a failed payment may need a retry
or a refund.
