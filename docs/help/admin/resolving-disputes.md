# Resolving disputes

When a customer or merchant disputes an order, it lands on the **Disputes** page
(*Order Disputes*). This is where you investigate and decide the outcome fairly.

![Order disputes](/help/screenshots/admin/disputes.png)

---

## Review a dispute

1. On the **Disputes** list, click a dispute to open it.
2. Read the history - the customer's claim, the merchant's response, and any
   attached evidence (photos, notes).
3. Use the reply box to **reply to the customer, the merchant, or leave an
   internal note** (notes are for admins only and aren't shown to either party).

> 💡 Gather both sides before deciding. The order's GPS log, proof-of-delivery
> photo, and timestamps (on the **Requests** page) are your best evidence.

---

## Decide the outcome

When you're ready to resolve, choose one of:

| Outcome | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Full refund** | Refund the customer the full amount |
| **Partial refund** | Refund part of the amount |
| **Resolved (no refund)** | Close it in the merchant's/rider's favour |
| **Reject dispute** | Dismiss the dispute |

Add **resolution notes** (these are shown to the customer) explaining the
decision, then confirm.

> ⚠️ Refunds move real money. Make sure the evidence supports the outcome before
> you confirm - the decision is final and recorded in **Audit Logs**.
