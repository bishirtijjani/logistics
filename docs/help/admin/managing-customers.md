# Managing customers

The **Customers** page lists every customer account on the platform. Use it to
look up a customer, review their history, and take action when needed.

![Customers](/help/screenshots/admin/customers.png)

---

## Find a customer

Use the **search box** to find someone by **name, email, or phone**.

## Open a customer's profile

Click a customer to open their detail page, which has three tabs:

- **Requests** - every order they've placed
- **Transactions** - their wallet and payment history
- **Addresses** - their saved delivery addresses

This is the fastest way to verify a complaint - you can see exactly what they
ordered and what they were charged.

---

## Actions you can take

- **Suspend** an account (you'll enter a reason) if it's being abused, and
  unsuspend it later.
- **Adjust their wallet** where allowed - you'll be asked *"Why is this change
  being made?"*. Always give a clear reason; it's recorded.

> ⚠️ Wallet adjustments and suspensions are sensitive, real-money/real-account
> actions. Double-check you have the right customer, and always record the
> reason - it shows up in **Audit Logs**.
