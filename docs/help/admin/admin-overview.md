# Getting started with the admin dashboard

The iPrime admin dashboard is your control room for the whole platform —
merchants, riders, orders, money, and support. This guide orients you so you
know where everything lives.

---

## Logging in

Go to the admin URL and sign in with your **email** and **password** on the
**iPrime Admin** screen. If you forget your password, contact another admin to
reset it.

---

## The Dashboard

The home **Dashboard** gives you the platform at a glance:

![The admin dashboard](/help/screenshots/admin/dashboard.png)

- **Six headline numbers** — Total Requests, Active Requests, Total Revenue,
  Merchants, Riders, and Users.
- **Revenue Trends** and **Request Trends** charts.
- Quick action panels: **Pending Merchants**, **Failed Transactions**, and
  **Pending Riders** — each with a **View All** link to jump straight to the
  full screen.

> 💡 Start your shift here. The pending panels tell you what needs attention
> right now (merchants and riders waiting for approval, payments that failed).

---

## Finding your way around

Everything is in the left sidebar, grouped by what you do:

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | The overview above |
| **Requests** | Live and past delivery orders |
| **Merchants** | Approve and manage shops |
| **Categories** | The merchant category list |
| **Riders** | Approve and manage riders |
| **Analytics** | Platform-wide reports |
| **Financial** | Reconciliation and money operations |
| **Disputes** | Customer/merchant order disputes |
| **Support** | Support tickets |
| **Marketing** | Banners and campaigns |
| **Waitlist** | Pre-launch sign-ups |
| **Safety** | Safety & compliance |
| **VTU Plans / Networks** | Airtime, data, cable, electricity pricing |
| **Customers** | All customer accounts |
| **Audit Logs** | A record of every admin action |
| **Transactions** | Every money movement |
| **Settings** | Platform-wide configuration |

At the **bottom of the sidebar** you'll find your name, a **light/dark toggle**,
and **Logout**.

> ⚠️ Admin actions are powerful and **logged**. Approving, suspending, refunding,
> and changing settings all affect real merchants, riders, and money — and every
> action is recorded in **Audit Logs**.
