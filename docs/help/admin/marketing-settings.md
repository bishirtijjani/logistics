# Marketing & platform settings

Two screens for shaping the platform: **Marketing** (what customers see) and
**Settings** (how the platform behaves).

---

## Marketing

The **Marketing** page (*Marketing Oversight*) is where you manage the banners and
promotional content shown to customers in the app.

![Marketing oversight](/help/screenshots/admin/marketing.png)

Create, edit, schedule, and remove banners. Keep them current — expired or
broken promos are the first thing customers notice.

---

## Platform settings

The **Settings** page controls platform-wide behaviour, in three sections:

![Platform settings](/help/screenshots/admin/settings.png)

- **Platform Configuration** — core fees and rules (delivery fares, withdrawal
  limits and fees, minimums, and similar).
- **Commission Defaults** — the default commission rate applied to new merchants
  (individual merchants can be overridden on their Merchant → Commission tab).
- **Notification Settings** — which alerts the platform sends, e.g. **New Order
  Alerts**.

> ⚠️ Settings here affect **everyone** — every order, payout, and merchant. Change
> one value at a time, and note what you changed and why. Everything is recorded
> in **Audit Logs**.

> 💡 Your own admin account details (name, password) are under **Profile**, not
> here.
