# Getting help: support and disputes

Stuck on something, or a customer has raised an issue? The **Support** page is
your direct line to the iPrime team. It has four tabs: **My Tickets**,
**Disputes**, **FAQ**, and **Knowledge Base**.

![The Support page](/help/screenshots/merchant/support.png)

---

## Raise a support ticket

When you need help from the platform team:

1. On the **My Tickets** tab, tap **"New ticket."**

![Creating a support ticket](/help/screenshots/merchant/support-new-ticket.png)

2. Pick a **Category** (Account, Payment, Order, Delivery, Product, Technical, or
   Other) and a **Priority**.
3. Add a short **Subject** and **describe the issue** in as much detail as you
   can.
4. Optionally **attach a screenshot** — it really speeds things up.
5. Tap **"Create ticket."**

Your tickets are listed with their status (**Open**, **In Progress**,
**Resolved**, and so on). Tap any ticket to read replies and continue the
conversation.

> 💡 The more detail and a screenshot you add up front, the faster we can sort it
> out.

---

## Disputes

If a customer disputes one of your orders, it appears under the **Disputes** tab.
Tap a dispute to see the details and **respond with your side** — add any
evidence you have (photos, notes). The platform team reviews both sides and
decides fairly.

> 💡 Respond to disputes promptly and politely with clear evidence — it protects
> your rating and your earnings.

---

## FAQ and Knowledge Base

Before raising a ticket, check the **FAQ** and **Knowledge Base** tabs — most
common questions are answered there instantly, no waiting required.

---

You can also reach us anytime by email at **support@iprime.ng**.
