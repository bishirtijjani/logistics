# Your shop settings

The **Settings** page is where you keep your shop details up to date and manage
your account. It's organised into four tabs: **Profile**, **Notifications**,
**Security**, and **Login History**.

---

## Profile

![Your shop profile settings](/help/screenshots/merchant/settings-profile.png)

This is your shop's public information. Here you can update:

- **Business name**, **email**, and **phone**
- **Location** — drag the pin to move where customers find you
- **Opening** and **Closing** times
- **Open for orders** — a switch to take your shop online or offline
- **Description**

Tap **"Save Changes"** when you're done.

> ⚠️ **"Open for orders"** is important: when it's **off**, your shop is hidden
> from customers and won't receive new orders. Turn it off if you need to close
> early or take a break, and back on when you're ready again.

> 💡 Your **business category** is shown here but can't be changed yourself —
> contact support if you need it changed.

---

## Notifications

Choose how you want to be told about activity:

- **Email Notifications** and **Push Notifications** (in your browser)
- **New Order Alerts** — get pinged the moment an order arrives
- **Order Status Alerts** — updates as orders progress

Keep **New Order Alerts** on so you never miss an order. Tap **"Save Changes"**
to apply.

---

## Security

![Changing your password](/help/screenshots/merchant/settings-security.png)

Change your password here. Enter your **current password**, then your **new
password** twice. A strength meter helps you pick a strong one — use a mix of
letters, numbers, and symbols.

> 💡 Use a password you don't use anywhere else, and never share it.

---

## Login History

See recent **logins** (with date, location, and device) and the **devices**
currently signed in to your account. If you spot a login you don't recognise,
change your password straight away under **Security**, and contact support.
