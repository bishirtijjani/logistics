# Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions merchants ask. Can't find yours? Raise
a ticket from **Support**, or email **support@**.

---

## Getting started & access

### Customers can't see my shop

Work through this checklist - it's almost always one of these:

1. **Are you approved?** New accounts are reviewed first (1–2 business days). You
   can't appear or take orders until you're approved.
2. **Is your location pinned?** If you skipped pinning your shop on the map,
   customers can't find you. Set it in **Settings → Profile**.
3. **Is "Open for orders" on?** In **Settings → Profile**, make sure the **Open
   for orders** switch is on.
4. **Are you within your opening hours?** Customers can only order between your
   opening and closing times.

### My account is "pending approval" - how long?

Usually **1–2 business days**. We email you the moment you're approved, and then
you can log in and finish setting up.

### I can't log in / "No merchant account is associated with this email"

This means the email isn't linked to a merchant account. Double-check you're
using the email you **registered as a merchant** with, and that your account has
been **approved**.

### I didn't get my email verification code

Codes arrive within a minute. If not: check your **spam** folder, then tap
**"Resend code"** (you can resend once every 60 seconds). Make sure the email is
spelled correctly.

### I forgot my password

On the login screen tap **"Forgot your password?"** and we'll email you a reset
link. Already logged in? Change it under **Settings → Security**.

## Money & withdrawals

### A withdrawal is still pending

**Standard** withdrawals (free) usually arrive within about a day; **instant**
ones (small fee) within minutes. Check **Earnings → Withdrawal History** for the
status. If a standard withdrawal hasn't landed after a couple of business days,
raise a support ticket.

### The "Request Withdrawal" button is greyed out

That happens when your **Available Balance is ₦0**. Earnings sit in **Pending**
until each order is delivered and settled, then move to **Available** - that's
what you can withdraw.

### What are the withdrawal limits?

Minimum **₦1,000** and maximum **₦1,000,000** per request.

### Why is my balance lower than the order totals?

A small platform **commission** is deducted from each sale - you can see the
total under **Total Commission** on the Earnings page.

## Products & orders

### The "New Product" button is greyed out

It's just loading your shop's category - wait a second or two and it'll become
clickable.

### I've sold out of an item

Don't delete it. On the **Products** page, tap **"Available"** on the item to
flip it to **Hidden**, then flip it back when you restock. Deleting is permanent.

### A new order isn't showing up

Check the **connection indicator** at the top-right of the **Orders** page - it
should be green. Tap the **refresh** button, or reload the page.

### I need to temporarily stop taking orders

Turn off **Open for orders** in **Settings → Profile**. Your shop goes offline
until you turn it back on.

### A customer raised a dispute against my order

Open it under **Support → Disputes**, and respond with your side and any evidence
(photos, notes). The team reviews both sides fairly. Respond promptly to protect
your rating.

## Account

### How do I change my business category?

You can't change it yourself - contact **support@** and we'll update it
for you.

### I saw a login I don't recognise

Change your password immediately under **Settings → Security**, then email
**support@** so we can help secure your account.
