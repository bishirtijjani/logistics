# Getting paid: earnings and withdrawals

Every sale you make lands in your Logistics Platform wallet. The **Earnings** page shows what
you've made and lets you cash out to your bank.

![Your Earnings page](/help/screenshots/merchant/earnings.png)

---

## Understanding your balances

At the top you'll see four cards:

| Card | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Available Balance** | Money you can withdraw **right now** |
| **Pending Balance** | Earnings from orders still being completed (settling soon) |
| **Total Earnings** | Everything you've earned, all-time |
| **Total Commission** | The platform fee already deducted from your sales |

> 💡 Money moves from **Pending** to **Available** once an order is delivered and
> settled. Then it's yours to withdraw.

---

## Withdraw to your bank

1. Tap **"Request Withdrawal."**

![The withdrawal form](/help/screenshots/merchant/earnings-withdraw.png)

2. Enter the **amount**.
3. Choose how fast you want it:
   - **Standard** - **free**, arrives within about a day.
   - **Instant** - arrives in minutes, for a small fee (about 2%).
4. Enter your **bank**, **account number**, and **account name**.
5. Confirm.

You can follow the status anytime under **"Withdrawal History."**

> ⚠️ Withdrawals are a **minimum of ₦1,000** and a **maximum of ₦1,000,000** per
> request. The **"Request Withdrawal"** button is greyed out when your available
> balance is ₦0.

---

## Refunds and disputes

From the Earnings page you can also:

- **Process Refund** - issue a refund to a customer for an order.
- **Refund History** - see every refund you've made.
- **Disputes** - review and respond to any payment disputes.

You can export your transactions to CSV or PDF anytime for your own records using
the date-range tools further down the page.
