# Receiving and fulfilling orders

Everything that happens after a customer hits "order" lives on the **Orders**
page. This guide walks you through the full journey - from a new order arriving
to a rider picking it up.

![Your Orders page](/help/screenshots/merchant/orders.png)

---

## How an order moves

Every order moves through a few clear stages. You handle the first three; Logistics Platform
handles the rest.

1. **Requested** - a new order just came in. *(You: accept or reject it.)*
2. **Accepted** - you've taken the order. *(You: start preparing.)*
3. **Processing** - you're making it. *(You: mark it ready when packed.)*
4. **Ready for Pickup** - a rider is called to collect it.
5. **Rider Assigned → Delivered** - Logistics Platform's rider takes it from here.

New orders appear **instantly** - watch the little connection indicator at the
top-right; when it's green, you're live and receiving in real time.

---

## 1. Accept a new order

When an order shows **"Requested"** (yellow badge), open it and choose:

- Tap the green **check** to **Accept** it, or
- Tap the **✕** to **Reject** it (you'll be asked for a reason).

> 💡 Accept quickly - customers see how fast you respond, and a speedy kitchen
> gets more orders.

## 2. Start preparing

Once accepted, tap **"Process"**. The order moves to **Processing** - this tells
the customer you're making their order right now.

## 3. Mark it ready

When the order is packed and ready, tap **"Ready"**. It becomes **"Ready for
Pickup"** and a rider is automatically dispatched to collect it.

> ⚠️ Only mark an order **Ready** when it's genuinely packed - a rider is sent the
> moment you do.

That's your part done. You'll see the order update to **Rider Assigned** and
then **Delivered** on its own.

---

## Other things you can do with an order

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 👁 **View Details** | See the full order - items, customer, delivery address |
| 🖨 **Print Receipt** | Print a paper receipt for the order |
| ✏️ **Modify** | Adjust items or add a note (before pickup) |
| **Cancel** | Cancel an accepted order if you truly can't fulfil it |

## Finding orders

- **Search** by order ID or customer name.
- Use the **status filter** to show only, say, *Requested* or *Delivered*.
- **Advanced Filters** let you narrow by date range or order value.
- **Export** downloads your orders as a CSV or PDF for your records.

> 💡 Got lots of orders at once? Tick several with the checkboxes and use **Accept
> All** to take them in one go.
