# Set up your shop

Welcome to iPrime! This guide takes you from sign-up all the way to your first
sale — it only takes about **5 minutes**. You'll create your account, get
approved, pin your shop on the map, set your hours, and add your first product.

---

## 1. Create your account

On the sign-up page, tap **"Register as a Merchant"** and fill in the three short
steps:

![Sign up — your personal details (step 1 of 3)](/help/screenshots/merchant/01-signup-step1.png)

- **Step 1 — About you:** your first name, last name, email, phone number, and a
  password (at least 8 characters).
- **Step 2 — About your business:** pick the **category** that fits your shop
  (Food, Pharmacy, Groceries, and so on), then your business name and contact.
- **Step 3 — Your hours:** set the times you open and close.

Then tap **"Complete Registration."**

> 💡 **Tip:** Use a phone number you actually answer — it's how customers and
> riders reach you about orders.

---

## 2. Verify your email

We send a **6-digit code** to the email you signed up with. Type it into the
**"Verification Code"** box and tap **"Verify Email."**

![Enter the 6-digit code we email you](/help/screenshots/merchant/02-verify-email.png)

> 💡 **Didn't get it?** Wait a minute, check your spam folder, then tap
> **"Resend code"** (you can resend once every 60 seconds).

---

## 3. Wait for approval

After you verify, your account is **reviewed by our team before it goes live** —
this usually takes **1–2 business days**. We'll email you as soon as you're
approved.

> ⚠️ **Heads-up:** You can't open your dashboard until you're approved. That's
> normal — it keeps the marketplace trustworthy for customers.

---

## 4. Log in

Once you're approved, go to the login page, enter your **email** and
**password**, and tap **"Sign In."**

> 💡 Forgot your password? Tap **"Forgot your password?"** and we'll email you a
> reset link.

---

## 5. Pin your shop on the map

The first time you log in, a **"Set up your business"** wizard opens
automatically. The most important step is your location: **drag the pin to
exactly where your shop is.**

![Drag the pin to your exact shop location](/help/screenshots/merchant/03-wizard-location.png)

This is how customers find you and how we work out delivery fees, so take a
moment to get it right. When you see **"Location saved,"** tap **"Next."**

> ⚠️ **This step is required.** Your shop won't be visible to customers until
> your location is pinned.

---

## 6. Add your first product

Tap **"Add your first product"** to open the product form, then:

![Add your first product — name, photo, and price](/help/screenshots/merchant/05-wizard-product.png)

- **Details:** add a clear **photo**, a **name** (e.g. *Jollof Rice*), and a
  short **description**.
- **Pricing:** set the **price** and how much you have in **stock**. (If your
  item comes in sizes or options, you can add those too.)
- For food shops, you can add **choices** like protein or sides.

Tap **"Create product."** Back in the wizard you'll see **"First product
added!"** — then tap **"Complete Setup."**

---

## 🎉 You're live!

That's it — your shop is set up and ready to sell. From your dashboard you can:

- Watch orders arrive in real time under **Orders**
- Add more items under **Products**
- Track your money and cash out under **Earnings**
- Update your hours, location, and password under **Settings**
