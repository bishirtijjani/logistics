## 1. What this policy covers

This Refund Policy explains when and how **MH Prime** ("**Logistics Platform**",
"**we**", "**us**") refunds money you spent through the Logistics Platform customer
app, rider app, or merchant tools. It applies to every transaction
type the platform supports:

1. **Bill payments** - airtime, data, electricity tokens, TV subscriptions,
   and any future biller we add.
2. **Wallet operations** - top-ups, withdrawals, and peer-to-peer transfers.
3. **Delivery orders** - food, groceries, pharmacy, and parcel deliveries
   from independent merchants and riders.
4. **Cancellations** - orders cancelled by you, a rider, or a merchant before
   completion.

This policy sits alongside the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). Where the Terms grant a specific
right (for example, a chargeback through your bank), that right is
preserved regardless of what this policy says.

---

## 2. The principle

> **If we didn't deliver what you paid for, you get your money back -
> usually automatically, and usually to your Logistics Platform wallet within
> minutes.**

We don't make you wait, fill in long forms, or chase support for
refunds the system can detect on its own. Where a human review is needed,
the SLA is published below and we stick to it.

---

## 3. Automatic refunds (no support ticket needed)

The following scenarios trigger an automatic refund to your Logistics Platform
wallet. You'll receive an in-app notification and a transaction-ledger
entry confirming the reversal.

| Trigger | Refund to | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|
| Airtime / data bundle vend fails at the telco | Wallet | Within 60 seconds |
| Electricity token not issued by the disco | Wallet | Within 60 seconds |
| TV subscription vend declined by the provider | Wallet | Within 60 seconds |
| Wallet-to-wallet transfer to an invalid recipient | Wallet | Instant |
| Bank withdrawal rejected by destination bank | Wallet | Within 5 minutes |
| Order cancelled by the merchant before pickup | Wallet | Within 60 seconds |
| Order cancelled by you before a rider is assigned | Wallet | Instant |

If you don't see the refund within the window above, contact us at
**contact@** with the transaction ID - we'll investigate the
same business day.

## 4. Refunds that need a quick human review

Some refunds need a person to confirm what happened. We aim to close
these within the SLA below; the clock starts when you submit the
request.

| Scenario | SLA | What we ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong item delivered, or missing item from a merchant order | 24 hours | A photo of what arrived + the order ID |
| Damaged item on arrival | 24 hours | A photo of the damage + the order ID |
| Rider failed to deliver after pickup (food spoiled, parcel lost) | 48 hours | The order ID and a short description |
| Wallet top-up not credited despite a successful bank transfer | 24 hours | The bank transfer reference / receipt |
| Unauthorised transaction on your wallet | 24 hours | Confirmation that you didn't approve it, and any details (time, device) |

To raise one of these, open the order in the customer app and tap
**"Get help with this order"**, or email **contact@** with the
transaction ID. Once approved, the refund hits your wallet within 60
seconds.

## 5. Withdrawing the refund to a bank account

Refunds always land in your Logistics Platform wallet first. From the wallet, you
can:

- Spend the balance on any product on the platform (deliveries, bills,
  P2P transfers), or
- Withdraw it to any Nigerian bank account in your name.

Withdrawals use our payment partner's instant transfer rail and
typically arrive within 60 seconds. Bank-side delays are outside our
control; if a withdrawal hasn't arrived after 30 minutes, we'll trace
and resolve it under the **Automatic refunds** policy above.

## 6. Cancellations

### 6.1 Customer-initiated cancellations

| When you cancel | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before the merchant accepts the order | 100% to wallet, instant |
| After the merchant accepts but before a rider is assigned | 100% to wallet, less a small platform fee where it has already been incurred |
| After a rider has picked the order up | Not eligible - the goods have already been prepared / dispatched. Raise a help ticket if there's a quality issue when the order arrives. |

### 6.2 Merchant- or rider-initiated cancellations

If a merchant runs out of stock or a rider abandons a job mid-route,
the order is cancelled at no fee to you and we refund 100% to your
wallet automatically.

### 6.3 Force majeure

We may pause or cancel orders during events outside our control -
violent weather, network outages, public emergencies, government
restrictions. In every such case, customers are refunded 100% to the
wallet and we don't charge cancellation fees on either side.

## 7. What is **not** refundable

- **Successfully delivered orders.** If you receive what you ordered in
  good condition, the transaction is final.
- **Successfully vended bills.** Airtime, data, tokens, or TV
  subscriptions delivered to the wrong number / meter / smartcard
  *that you entered yourself* are not refundable - the system has no
  way to undo a successful vend on the operator's side. Always double-check
  the recipient before confirming.
- **Tips paid to riders.** Once a tip is paid, it belongs to the rider.
- **Promotional credits and referral bonuses.** These are platform
  credits, not money you paid in - they cannot be withdrawn or
  refunded, only spent.
- **Disputes raised more than 30 days after the transaction.** We keep
  detailed transaction logs but the operator-side evidence needed to
  resolve a refund typically expires after 30 days.

## 8. Chargebacks and bank disputes

If you funded your wallet by card or bank transfer and want to dispute
that funding through your bank, you're entitled to do so under your
bank's rules - this policy does not remove that right. However:

- Raising a chargeback before contacting **contact@** delays
  resolution by weeks. We can almost always refund a legitimate complaint
  in-app within 24 hours.
- Chargebacks that turn out to be fraudulent (i.e. you received the value
  and disputed the funding to keep it) will lead to immediate account
  suspension and may be reported to the relevant authorities.

## 9. How to escalate

If you're not happy with how a refund was handled:

1. Reply to the original support thread asking for an escalation.
2. If still unresolved, email **contact@** with the transaction ID
   and a summary. A senior team member responds within 3 business days.
3. If you remain unsatisfied, you have the right to refer the matter to
   the **Central Bank of Nigeria Consumer Protection Department** (for
   wallet, withdrawal, or bill-payment disputes) or to the **Federal
   Competition and Consumer Protection Commission** (for delivery /
   merchant disputes).
