## 4. The wallet

### 4.1 What it is
Your iPrime wallet is a prepaid balance tied to your verified identity.
You can fund it via bank transfer to your dedicated virtual account
(NUBAN), transfer to another iPrime user, withdraw to a Nigerian bank
account, and spend it on iPrime services (deliveries, merchant orders,
VTU top-ups).

### 4.2 Funding & withdrawal
- Funding via NUBAN is credited as soon as our payment partner confirms the inbound transfer (usually instant; allow up to 30 minutes for delays at the partner bank).
- Withdrawals are subject to KYC level, daily limits (configurable in the app under wallet limits), and our payment partner's processing times — usually under 5 minutes during business hours.
- Withdrawal fees, if any, are shown on the withdrawal screen before you confirm.

### 4.3 Escrow during deliveries
When you place a merchant order, the cost (items + delivery fee + service
charge) is moved from your wallet into escrow. We release funds to the
merchant and rider only when the order is marked **DELIVERED**. If the
order is cancelled before delivery, the unused portion is refunded to
your wallet per §6 below.

### 4.4 Currency, holds, and disputes
- All amounts are in **Nigerian Naira (NGN)**.
- We may temporarily hold or freeze a wallet balance if we reasonably suspect fraud, money laundering, sanctions risk, or a chargeback dispute — and for no longer than is necessary to investigate.
- You will not earn interest on wallet balances; iPrime is not a bank.

## 5. Placing orders & requests

### 5.1 Quotes are binding
Before you confirm any request, we show you a quote (items, delivery fee,
service charge, total). The quote is server-signed and valid for **5
minutes**. If you take longer to confirm, the quote refreshes — prices
may change if merchant prices or delivery distance have changed.

### 5.2 Confirmation creates a contract
Confirming a quote:
- Authorizes us to move funds from your wallet into escrow
- Forms a contract between you and the merchant for the goods (where applicable)
- Forms a contract between you and the assigned rider for the delivery

### 5.3 Pharmacy orders
For pharmacy items flagged as prescription-only, you must upload a valid
prescription at checkout. The merchant pharmacist reviews it before
preparing the order. Submitting a forged prescription is illegal and is
grounds for immediate suspension and reporting.

### 5.4 Substitutions (grocery)
For grocery orders, you can pick a substitution preference per line
item: **Do not substitute**, **Merchant's pick**, or **Contact me**. The
merchant follows your preference; if they cannot, the unfulfilled portion
is refunded.

### 5.5 Age-restricted goods
We do not currently sell age-restricted goods (alcohol, tobacco,
prescription drugs without a prescription). Merchants who list such items
are removed.

## 6. Cancellation & refunds

### 6.1 Customer cancellation
You can cancel an order before **PICKED_UP**. After that, the goods are
in transit and you must accept delivery or refuse it at the door (in
which case you may be charged a delivery fee but refunded for items).

A small **cancellation fee** may apply depending on how far along the
order is when you cancel — the fee is shown in the cancellation preview
before you confirm. Once you confirm cancellation:
- The cancellation fee (if any) is debited from your wallet.
- The remainder of your payment is refunded to your wallet immediately.

### 6.2 Rider-initiated cancellation
A rider may cancel before pickup for operational reasons (customer
unreachable, wrong address, vehicle issue). No fee is charged to you;
your full payment is refunded.

### 6.3 Auto-cancellation
If no rider accepts within the matching window, the order is
auto-cancelled and refunded in full.

### 6.4 Disputes
If something goes wrong (item not received, item damaged, wrong item,
partial delivery, payment issue, rider/merchant behaviour), open a
dispute in the app within **7 days** of the issue. Our support team
reviews the evidence (photos, message history, GPS data) and may:
- Refund all or part of the order
- Side with the merchant/rider if evidence supports them
- Escalate to law enforcement for clear fraud

Decisions are final after support review unless new evidence emerges.
