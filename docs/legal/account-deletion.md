# Delete your iPrime account

**Last updated:** {{TODAY}}

This page explains how to delete your **iPrime** account and what happens to
your data when you do. It applies to both the **iPrime customer app** and the
**iPrime rider app**, operated by **MH Prime**.

---

## How to delete your account

### Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

1. Open the iPrime app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted immediately once the checks
   below pass.

### Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email **bishirtijjani247@gmail.com** from the
email address on your account with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will
verify your identity and complete the deletion for you.

---

## Before you can delete

For your protection, deletion is blocked while either of these is true:

- **You have active deliveries.** Complete or cancel any in-progress orders or
  trips first.
- **Your wallet has a balance.** Withdraw your remaining funds to your bank
  account first, so no money is lost.

---

## What is deleted

As soon as your account is deleted, we permanently remove or anonymize your
**personal data**, including:

- Your name, email address, and phone number
- Your saved addresses
- Your profile photo
- Your transaction PIN
- Your push-notification token and active login sessions
- Your KYC verification status

Your account is then deactivated and can no longer be used to sign in.

## What is kept, and for how long

Because iPrime provides a wallet and processes payments, Nigerian law (including
**Central Bank of Nigeria** rules and the **Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023**)
requires us to retain certain **financial and transaction records** even after
your account is deleted. These records are kept in a **de-identified** form
(no longer linked to your name or contact details) for the retention period
required by law, after which they are deleted. See the retention section of our
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for full details.
